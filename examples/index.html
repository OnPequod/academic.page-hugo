Academic.page Hugo starter: example sites

Each site below is built by the Academic.page Hugo starter from content written in Academic.page. The starter reads the content through the Academic.page content API when it builds, and these sites are rebuilt every day.

  • Personal site: a researcher's profile, posts, publications, talks, projects, and teaching.
  • Lab site: a research group with its members, alumni, projects, and publications.
  • Course site: a course with its offerings, syllabus, lessons, assignments, and readings.
  • Study site: a research study with its details, study team, materials, and outputs.

The people, groups, courses, and studies on these sites are made up, and most of their text is placeholder text.
